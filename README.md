# **My Demon (마이데몬)**
_"He's a demon, but I think... I've fallen for him."_

## Summary
My Demon is a 2023-2024 South Korean fantasy romance series that aired on Netflix. The story revolves around a cynical, workaholic heiress named Do Do-hee and a centuries-old, powerful demon named Jung Gu-won.

Gu-won thrives by making tempting deals with desperate humans, collecting their souls after ten years. His life takes a chaotic turn when he gets entangled with Do Do-hee, a woman who is surrounded by enemies and distrusts everyone. In a fateful encounter, Gu-won's demonic powers—symbolized by a cross tattoo on his wrist—are suddenly transferred to Do-hee.

To prevent his own extinction (as his powers are his life source) and to protect his new "power bank," Gu-won enters into a contract marriage with Do-hee. What starts as a relationship of convenience and mutual benefit slowly blossoms into an unexpected and dangerous romance.

## Main Cast
- **Kim Yoo-jung** as **Do Do-hee** (The cold-hearted heiress of Mirae Group)

- **Song Kang** as **Jung Gu-won** (The charming but lethal demon who loses his powers)

- **Lee Sang-yi** as **Joo Seok-hoon** (Do Do-hee's relative and CEO of Mirae Investment)

- **Kim Hae-sook** as **Joo Cheon-sook** (The founder of Mirae Group and Do-hee's adoptive mother)

### Key Themes
1. **Fantasy & Romance:** The core of the show is the "impossible" love story between a human and a supernatural being.

2. **Contractual Relationships:** The classic K-drama trope of a fake relationship (in this case, a contract marriage) that leads to real feelings.

3. **Power Dynamics:** Explores who truly holds the power—the demon who needs his abilities back, or the human who now wields them.

4. **Redemption & Fate:** The series delves into the demon's past and his chance at finding redemption through love.
